DJ Harmonic — Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 31, 2026

Data Controller: Andrea Boschin — privacy@boschin.it

In short

DJ Harmonic runs entirely on your device. It has no account and no sign-in, and the developer operates no server of their own. The tracks you analyse, the results of those analyses, and your settings stay on your device: the developer never receives them.

The app displays no advertising and contains no analytics, tracking, profiling, or crash-reporting service — neither the developer's own nor anyone else's.

DJ Harmonic is a paid app. The purchase is handled by the store you bought it from — Google Play or the Apple App Store — which is the only third party involved in the ordinary use of the app.

What stays on your device

DataWhere it is stored
The tracks you add to the archive, the tags read from their files (title, artists, album, year), the detected key and the other results of the analysisLocal SQLite database
Language, theme, note notation, harmonic rule set and lock, analysis depthLocal app preferences

The developer does not keep a copy of this data on any server, and has no way of reading it.

Uninstalling DJ Harmonic removes the copy of the app data stored on your device. If Android backup is enabled, however, the operating system may include some app data in the backup associated with your Google account. That copy is managed by Google under its own terms, and the developer of DJ Harmonic does not have access to it.

The music files you choose

To analyse a track, DJ Harmonic asks you to pick an audio file through your device's own file picker. The app receives access to the file you selected, and to that file alone — not to your music library as a whole, and not to the other files on the device.

The file is opened and decoded on the device, in order to read its tags and examine its audio content. Its content is not uploaded anywhere, is not copied to any server, and is never disclosed to the developer or to third parties. What the app keeps is a reference to the file and the outcome of the analysis, not a copy of the music.

Connections to the outside

Analysis — key detection, and everything that goes with it — is carried out by your device's own processor. The app contains no analytics, advertising, or crash-reporting SDK, and sends the developer no information about how you use it.

Only two things involve someone else, and both are described below: the purchase, which is handled by the store, and the web version, which by its nature is downloaded from a server.

Purchase and the app stores

DJ Harmonic is sold as a paid app. The purchase, the payment, and the receipt are handled entirely by Google Play or the Apple App Store, depending on where you obtained the app.

The developer does not receive your card details or any other payment information. From the store the developer receives only what stores make available to sellers: essentially sales reports and, for an individual order, an order identifier and the country of purchase.

Google and Apple process the data connected with the transaction as independent controllers, under their own terms and privacy policies.

Your purchase is associated with your Google or Apple account and can therefore be restored from there if you reinstall the app or change device. Verifying it is a matter between your device and the store: nothing about the purchase reaches the developer.

Permissions

DJ Harmonic does not request access to your location, your contacts, the camera, or the microphone.

To open a track it relies on the system file picker, which hands the app the single file you have chosen; on Android this requires no general storage permission.

Using DJ Harmonic in a browser

Where DJ Harmonic is offered as a web version, the app is downloaded from the server that hosts it. As with any request made over the Internet, that server may receive your device's IP address and standard information about your browser — the ordinary log of a web server, not a tracking system.

Once loaded, the app behaves exactly as it does on a phone: the files you pick are read inside the browser, the archive and the settings are saved in the browser's local storage on your own device, and nothing is transmitted to the developer.

Children

DJ Harmonic is not specifically designed as a service for children.

The app shows no advertising, creates no user account, and collects no personal data: there is therefore no profiling of users, whatever their age.

This website

This website — the pages you are reading now — sets no cookies, loads no fonts, scripts, or images from other servers, and uses no analytics or advertising services. Nothing about your visit reaches the developer.

One single item is saved in your browser's local storage, and nowhere else:

ItemWhy
djharmonic.langThe language you choose with the switch at the top of the page, so the site opens in it on your next visit. It is written only when you pick a language yourself: the automatic detection of your browser's language saves nothing.

It is strictly necessary to provide what you asked for and, under Article 5(3) of Directive 2002/58/EC, does not require consent. It contains no identifier, it is shared with no one, and you can delete it at any time by clearing the site data in your browser.

The pages are hosted on GitHub Pages which — like any hosting service — keeps its own web-server logs. The links to the app stores are ordinary links: nothing is requested from Google or Apple until you open one.

Deleting your data

The data managed by DJ Harmonic is stored on your device and can be deleted without contacting the developer: there is no copy held elsewhere that would need to be erased.

You can:

  • uninstall the app, which removes its local data from your device;
  • on Android, use Settings → Apps → DJ Harmonic → Storage → Clear data to remove local data while keeping the app installed;
  • in the browser version, clear the site data for the page the app is loaded from.

If you use Android backup, a copy of some data may remain in the backup associated with your Google account and is managed directly by Google.

The record of the purchase is held by the store and follows the rules of your Google or Apple account.

Legal basis

Where European data protection law applies, the limited processing described in this policy may rely on the following legal bases:

  • performance of a contract — Article 6(1)(b) GDPR — for the purchase of the app and its management by the store, and for providing the app itself;
  • legitimate interests — Article 6(1)(f) GDPR — where applicable, for the security and proper operation of the services involved, such as the ordinary logs of the server that delivers the web version or this website.

None of the processing described here relies on consent, because the app carries no advertising, analytics, or profiling.

The stores and the hosting provider mentioned in this policy may also process data as independent controllers, on the legal bases described in their own privacy policies.

Your rights

DJ Harmonic does not use user accounts, and the developer holds no copy of your tracks, analyses, or settings on any server.

For this reason there is, at the developer's end, no personal record to be accessed, corrected, exported, or deleted on your behalf: that data lives on your device and is managed there.

Where European data protection law applies, the rights provided under Articles 15 to 22 GDPR remain applicable where relevant, including access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, data portability, and objection.

For any request concerning processing carried out in connection with DJ Harmonic, you can contact privacy@boschin.it.

For data processed directly by the parties named above — Google, Apple, and the provider hosting this website — please refer to their respective privacy policies and, where necessary, contact them directly.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority. In Italy, the competent authority is the Garante per la protezione dei dati personali; you may also contact the authority responsible in the country where you live or work.

Changes to this policy

Any change to this privacy policy will be published on this page together with an updated revision date.

The version in force at any given time is always available on this page, whose address is indicated in the app's listing on the store. You are encouraged to consult it periodically.
